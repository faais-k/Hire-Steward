# JobPilot — Application Safety and External Action Policy

**Status:** Normative. This policy is enforced in backend code; UI and AI prompts are not enforcement mechanisms.

## 1. Purpose

Protect the candidate from incorrect, duplicate, unauthorized, misleading, or legally consequential external actions. The system should automate clerical work, not make unsupported representations on the user's behalf.

## 2. Pre-submit gate sequence

Immediately before every external submission, the backend must evaluate all gates against a frozen snapshot. Suggested order:

1. Target source is enabled and current capability record allows the exact action.
2. Destination domain and requisition identity match the approved job/source record.
3. Job remains live/published or source confirms it is valid.
4. Exact vacancy duplicate check passes.
5. Company/role cooldown check passes or a verified new requisition exception applies.
6. Daily application/email caps and concurrency lock pass.
7. Mandatory qualifications have no unresolved mismatch requiring review.
8. Compensation evidence has trusted provenance and satisfies the persisted user minimum (INR/year); any unverified report, unknown, non-normalizable, conflicting, or threshold-straddling compensation is investigated/reviewed. A trusted confirmed unpaid classification is blocked independently of the configured minimum.
9. Work authorization/sponsorship is not confirmed incompatible; unknown sponsorship has been investigated or explicitly held for user review.
10. Every application question has a valid, current, grounded answer.
11. Sensitive-data release to the source is within an explicit field/domain allowlist.
12. Required document versions exist and match the preview.
13. Legal declarations have been classified and any required approval recorded.
14. User authorization/consent for the selected automation level is still valid and covers this action.
15. Submission plan hash and current policy evaluation are persisted before the side effect.

If any gate is unknown, errored, stale, or conflicting, fail closed for automatic submission and route to review/manual mode. Safe discovery and unrelated preparation work may continue.

## 3. Eligibility rules

### Mandatory qualifications

- A confirmed mismatch or unresolved required field always prevents automatic submission and requires review.
- Human review may decide the mismatch is acceptable for the user to pursue, but it cannot authorize false answers.
- An experience gap alone is not a hard reject unless the job's qualification is a hard requirement and the facts confirm the user does not meet it; the user may still choose manual pursuit.

### Skills

- Do not require every listed skill.
- Separate required from preferred skills and responsibilities.
- Lack of resume evidence is not proof the candidate lacks a skill.
- Only represent a skill as possessed when supported by evidence or explicitly confirmed by the user.

### Compensation and contract type

- Never apply to an unpaid role, regardless of the configured minimum.
- The configurable minimum compensation preference defaults to INR 100,000 per year. The user may set any finite, non-negative INR amount, including zero. The setting is a preference, not permission to apply to unpaid roles.
- The backend must load and enforce the persisted setting through deterministic policy code. A reliable annualized amount below it fails the compensation gate; an amount at or above it passes this gate only. A score, frontend result, user-facing explanation generated by AI, or model output cannot override the result.
- Reported compensation status and evidence verification are separate facts. Caller-supplied status, amounts/ranges, units, and evidence references are unverified observations. A reference string or URL does not prove that a source was retrieved or supports the amount. Model-generated assertions are never verification. Unverified observations are `REVIEW_REQUIRED` and cannot proceed to application submission.
- A future controlled source-ingestion/verifier component must establish permitted source access, retrieve and retain source/version provenance and the supporting text/span, validate the reported amount, currency, period and paid status, and record verifier method/version/time before evidence can be trusted. No complete trusted evidence-ingestion path currently exists. Only that internal verification boundary may create trusted domain evidence; the public request schema cannot.
- A reported unpaid status without trusted provenance remains an unverified report and is not stated as a confirmed unpaid fact. It still cannot proceed because all unverified observations require review. A trusted, confirmed unpaid classification remains blocked regardless of threshold.
- Unknown salary: investigate and show uncertainty; do not treat it as a pass or a confirmed failure. Unresolved unknowns require review before submission.
- A reliable range entirely below the configured minimum fails this gate; a range entirely above passes this gate only; a range crossing the minimum requires review unless reliable additional evidence resolves it.
- Convert monthly, annual, hourly, daily, weekly, stipend, commission and contract rates only on a defensible documented basis. An explicitly recurring monthly rate may be annualized as monthly amount × 12, recording the assumption; this does not establish total earnings for a fixed-term contract. Hourly/daily/contract values require reliable hours, days or duration. Do not invent working time, contract duration or exchange rates.
- Compare non-INR compensation with the INR setting only when a reliable exchange rate, source, timestamp and conversion basis are recorded; otherwise review. Store the original amount, currency and period as well as any normalized value and assumptions.
- Setting changes must be versioned and audited with previous/new amount, currency and period, timestamp, actor and settings version. Keep audit metadata limited to the change; do not include unrelated sensitive information.
- Each assessment and application policy evaluation must retain the minimum amount/currency/annualized unit and relevant conversion assumptions used at that time. New settings apply to future/recomputed decisions; they do not silently rewrite earlier assessments or already-submitted application facts, answers or outcomes. Recheck the current persisted setting before preparation, scheduled actions and final submission.
- Commission-only or unclear variable pay requires review; do not assume a fixed base salary exists.
- Do not mistake currency symbols or local separators. Store source amount/currency/period as well as any annualized result and assumptions.

### Sponsorship / work authorization

- Unknown sponsorship is not an automatic reject; investigate and mark `UNKNOWN` until supported.
- Confirmed inability to work in the role's location, or confirmed sponsorship unavailability where it is required, may block automatic pursuit.
- Never infer citizenship, visa status or legal authorization from name, language, current location, or other proxies.
- Ask for current user-approved facts when a declaration requires them.

### Confidence

A strong relevance score cannot override low confidence, incomplete extraction, contradictory facts, unknown mandatory requirements or any hard gate. The system must show score and confidence separately.

## 4. Duplicate and cooldown policy

### Strong identity signals

1. ATS/source requisition ID + source/board identity.
2. Canonical vacancy URL and canonical job ID.
3. Stable external application reference.
4. Conservative normalized company + title + location + description hash and timing.

Weak similarity signals should raise possible duplicates for review, not merge unrelated jobs automatically.

- Exact vacancy already applied: hard block.
- Existing attempt with `SUBMITTING` or `CONFIRMATION_UNKNOWN`: hard block new submission until reconciled.
- Same company/similar role within 30 days: cooldown, except a verified genuinely new requisition can proceed to normal gates.
- Reposts and URL variants do not bypass the cooldown.
- Dedupe must be checked in a transaction immediately before creating the attempt, with DB-level uniqueness/locking where possible.

## 5. Submission state machine

Recommended states:

- `DISCOVERED`
- `ASSESSING`
- `BLOCKED`
- `REVIEW_REQUIRED`
- `ELIGIBLE`
- `PREPARING`
- `NEEDS_USER_INPUT`
- `READY_TO_SUBMIT`
- `SUBMITTING`
- `CONFIRMATION_UNKNOWN`
- `APPLIED`
- `FAILED_RETRYABLE`
- `MANUAL_ACTION_REQUIRED`
- `SKIPPED`

Transitions are server-validated. A direct jump from `DISCOVERED` to `APPLIED` is forbidden. `READY_TO_SUBMIT` is not equivalent to consent to submit: the current automation setting and explicit approval requirement still apply.

### Exactly-once caveat

The system cannot guarantee true exactly-once delivery across an arbitrary external website if the remote server accepts a submission but the client loses the response. The correct design is at-most-one active attempt plus reconciliation before retry. Do not claim exactly-once semantics where the external interface does not support idempotency keys or authoritative status lookups.

## 6. Crash and uncertain outcome procedure

1. Persist attempt ID, immutable plan, current policy evaluation and `SUBMITTING` state before external call.
2. Record request start and, where observable, request sent/response received.
3. On clear success with credible confirmation evidence, transition to `APPLIED` and store evidence/reference.
4. On clear rejection before acceptance, record failure with safe reason.
5. On timeout, connection reset, browser crash, ambiguous confirmation or process death after request may have been sent, set `CONFIRMATION_UNKNOWN`.
6. Reconcile through a permitted status endpoint, confirmation page/email or user review.
7. Retry only after evidence establishes the previous attempt did not submit and all policy checks are fresh.
8. Never convert generic HTTP 200, page navigation, button click, or absence of an error into `APPLIED` without defined source-specific confirmation evidence.

## 7. CAPTCHA and human-only steps

- Do not bypass CAPTCHA or anti-bot controls.
- Preserve the current form state and mark the affected attempt `MANUAL_ACTION_REQUIRED`.
- Ask the user to complete the challenge only when the site terms permit continued human-assisted workflow.
- If automation is disallowed, stop automated control and provide the official application link plus a prepared package for manual submission.
- Keep other permitted source discovery and unrelated preparation running.
- Never use stealth plugins, fingerprint spoofing, proxy rotation or account rotation to evade controls.

## 8. Browser automation boundary

- Explicit allowlist of permitted domains, source adapters and workflow capabilities.
- Use Playwright only where the site/source method has been reviewed and permitted.
- Do not allow model output to choose arbitrary URLs or run JavaScript.
- Use stable selectors and verify semantic page state before/after important actions.
- Validate field mapping and actual current form question text; sites can drift.
- Never fill hidden legal/identity fields by guessing.
- Block form submit if any visible required field is unknown, unapproved, or inconsistent with the frozen plan.
- Provide an emergency stop and adapter disable switch.

## 9. Recruiter email

### Send only when

- The listing explicitly labels the email as the application submission channel.
- Recipient domain/address is consistent with the target source or is user-approved.
- Message and attachments are grounded and reviewed by policy.
- Daily cap, duplicate-send check, consent, and data-release rules pass.

### Draft-only when

- The address is a general HR/contact address, an individual's public email with no instruction to apply there, or the purpose is ambiguous.
- Source instructions say to use a web form.
- Recipient authenticity is uncertain.

Default application-email limit: 5/day. Record exact recipient, subject, body hash, attachments/version hashes, provider response/message ID, and outcome. Do not send repeated generic emails or repeat follow-ups in a way that evades rate limits or annoys recipients. An uncertain send result must be reconciled before retry.

Gmail OAuth permissions are separate from ChatGPT's connected integrations. Use the narrowest feasible scope and verify current OAuth/app-review requirements. The Google scope documentation classifies `gmail.send` as sensitive: https://developers.google.com/workspace/gmail/api/auth/scopes.

## 10. Legal and high-stakes declarations

Questions or statements involving bonds, repayment commitments, non-competes, restrictive covenants, exclusivity, background-check consent, arbitration, financial liability, intellectual property assignments, or unclear material obligations need human review before acceptance.

Routine factual declarations may be automated only if:

- the statement text and version are captured;
- the underlying facts are verified and current;
- the user explicitly approved that category for automation;
- the policy review found no ambiguity or material obligation.

The system must not provide legal advice or represent that a clause is harmless. It should summarize the exact text in plain language, state uncertainty, and request user decision. No bond/material legal commitment is accepted without explicit approval.

## 11. Daily limits and pause controls

- Default maximum: 5 applications/day, configurable.
- Default maximum explicit application emails: 5/day, configurable.
- Counts use the configured timezone and documented day-boundary logic.
- Limits must be checked transactionally and against all in-progress/uncertain attempts; multiple tasks cannot race past the cap.
- Provide pause discovery, pause drafting, pause submission and global stop separately.
- A global stop prevents new side effects immediately; it must not erase pending reconciliation records.

## 12. Consent, revocation, and audit

Before enabling automation, the user sees which sources/actions may run, data sent, daily cap, review requirements, and how to stop. Store consent version/scope/time. Revocation blocks future new actions; it does not erase already completed actions or stop reconciliation of a previously uncertain external action.

Every side effect records actor, run ID, job snapshot ID, policy version, consent record, destination/source, attempt ID, document/answer hashes and evidence of the outcome. Logs are redacted.
